Five decisions. One clearer plan.

Use this guide to organize the choices that matter before work begins, so you can approach your renovation with a stronger sense of scope, priorities, and style.

01

Budget & planning

Define what you want to accomplish, set a budget, and outline the scope and timeline before speaking with contractors or designers.

02

Layout & design

Think through the room’s functionality, fixture placement, lighting changes, color scheme, and the overall mood you want to create.

03

Fixtures & finishes

Consider water efficiency, durability, maintenance, style, flooring, tile, countertops, and cabinetry together.

04

Lighting

Plan for overhead, sconce, and task lighting that supports both the look of the room and the way you use it.

05

Ventilation

Account for moisture control and proper ventilation to help protect the finished bathroom from mold growth.
